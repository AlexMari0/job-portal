# Job Portal

A job board where companies post job openings and job seekers browse and apply to them.

## Language

**Company**:
An organization that posts jobs, represented by a single login account per organization.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, employer, HR

**Candidate**:
A person looking for work who browses jobs and applies to them.
_Avoid_: User, applicant, job seeker

**Job**:
An opening posted by a Company that Candidates can apply to.
_Avoid_: Vacancy, posting, opening

**Hidden Job**:
A Job its Company has withdrawn from public view; it can be made visible again at any time.
_Avoid_: Inactive, closed, archived

**Application**:
A Candidate's application to a Job, with a status of Pending, Accepted, or Rejected.
_Avoid_: Job application, applicant
